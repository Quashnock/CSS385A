# Checkpoint 1: Project Proposal
By Aaron Quashnock

## Elevator Pitch
Are you a fan of good old-fashion carnival plinking games? Combine special bullets and other upgrades in this western-themed roguelite shooting gallery to get the highest score you can without going bankrupt. With a mix of good aim, solid strategy, and just the right amount of risk, you too can crush your previous records and become the best sharpshooter in the west!

## Game synopsis
This game is a western-themed roguelite shooting gallery where players shoot targets to get points, as well as money for bullets and upgrades. Upgrades will help players score more points over the course of a run in the game, but if the player runs out of money and bullets then their run is over. Scaling with how many points the player ends a run with, they gain a seperate currency used to purchase permanent upgrades that they can carry into subsequent runs.

### Objective
The objective is to shoot targets to score as many points as possible and improve their high scores across runs.

### Mechanics
Players are given a limited number of bullets in their gun's cylinder to shoot targets with at the start of a run. Shooting targets gives players money to use on upgrades and special bullets. Special bullets can include lucky shots that have a chance to apply a large multiplier to the target's score, or scatter bullets that break apart and spread before impact. Targets increase in variety, speed, difficulty and point worth as each run continues, so players will need to balance high-scoring bullets with ones that help them hit targets consistently. If money and bullets run out, then the run is over. Depending on the amount of points scored in a run, players receive a different currency that is used to purchase permanent upgrades that carry across runs. Permanent upgrades can include expansions to the gun's capacity and special bullet upgrades that further enhance their abilities.
